Questions

Plainly answered.

How is Sukyaan billed?

Per seat, per month. The team plan covers two seats in each of Finance, Sales, Procurement and Operations: eight seats at $31.25 each, $250 a month in total.

Is every feature included?

Yes. Every seat includes the full platform: graph, anomalies, controls, pipelines, dashboards, reports, Datic AI and the vendor portal.

Where is our data stored?

Only in India, on Datic's servers (Datic Cloud) or on your own server (self-hosted). You choose.

Is our data used to train AI?

Never. Your data is never used to train, fine-tune or evaluate any AI model, ours or anyone else's.

Can we stop at any time?

Yes, even mid-way through consulting. Work stops immediately and everything we hold is permanently destroyed within 15 days.
