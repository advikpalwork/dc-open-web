01Your role and ours

YouData Fiduciary

Personal data in your systems is yours. You decide its purpose and give individuals the notices the DPDPA requires.

DaticData Processor

We process that data only on your behalf, only on your documented instructions, and only for the scope agreed in writing.

02Two hosting models. You choose.

A · Datic Cloud

Sukyaan runs on Datic's servers in India. Each company's data is kept apart by the database itself, in storage reserved for you, encrypted with your own key.

B · Self-hosted

Sukyaan runs on your own server and all data stays there, under your control. The connector talks to it over your office network. Datic holds nothing during the subscription.

We prefer to host on your servers. Datic never imposes a storage location.

03How Sukyaan works with your systems

  • No migration. Sukyaan plugs into your ERP, CRM, Tally, Excel and other tools. Your original records stay where they are.
  • Read first, write only with approval. It writes back only through steps you have set up and approved.
  • A connector you can trust. Installed on your Tally or ERP computer, it connects over an encrypted channel, identifies itself with its own certificate and is linked to your company with a one-time pairing code.
  • No content on the connector. It keeps only a technical activity log, never your records.

06Security safeguards

Per-company encryptionFiles, bank details, PAN, Aadhaar, IFSC, phone numbers and emails sealed with AES-256-GCM, using your company's own key, itself protected by a master key kept outside the database.
Isolation by the databaseSeparation between companies is enforced by the database, with the platform connecting through a login that cannot bypass it.
Encrypted everywhereHTTPS/TLS on every connection; certificate-based identity for connectors.
Strong sign-inPasswords stored only as Argon2id hashes; optional two-step verification; role- and department-based access.
Full accountabilityActivity logs of sign-ins and important actions, plus a support-access log.
Tested recoveryNightly backups, test-restored every night, with an encrypted copy kept by a separate provider in India.

Self-hosted: the software ships with these safeguards; your server, network, backups and master key are under your control.

09Where data is processed, and AI

Client data is stored only in India, with backups in India, and never transferred to a country restricted under Section 16 of the DPDPA.

In IndiaAlways on

Reading scanned documents (Sarvam AI). Search runs on the same server as the platform, so documents are not sent elsewhere.

Outside IndiaYour choice

Reading document details and Datic AI answers use Anthropic's Claude, one task at a time, never stored for training. Switched off unless you permit it.

Your data will never be used to train AI. Not by Datic, not by any provider. Datic builds no foundation models and scrapes no public data. This commitment survives termination indefinitely.

10GDPR compliance

For clients and individuals in the EU, EEA and UK, Sukyaan meets the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation through the same safeguards described above.

Processor on your instructionsDatic acts only on your documented instructions, for the scope agreed in writing (Article 28).
Minimisation and storage limitsOnly what the service needs is kept, and every category is deleted on a fixed schedule (Article 5).
Security of processingPer-company AES-256-GCM encryption, database-enforced isolation, Argon2id passwords and tested backups (Article 32).
Breach notice in 24 hoursYou hear from us within 24 hours, well inside the 72-hour authority deadline (Article 33).
Data subject rightsAccess, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, actioned in the platform within 7 days of your request.
No transfers without your consentData leaves India only for AI features you permit, and is never used to train any model.

11Vendor portal

Datic Cloud

Vendors sign in with logins you create and see only the forms you give them. Only fields on your form are stored; bank account, PAN, Aadhaar, IFSC, phone and email are each encrypted with your key.

Self-hosted

The portal runs on your server behind a Cloudflare Tunnel, with no inbound firewall ports. Only portal pages are published; everything else is refused twice. Cloudflare stores nothing.

13Personal-data requests and governing law

When an individual asks you to access, correct, update or erase their personal data, or raises a grievance, we carry out whatever is needed in the platform within 7 days of your request. We never respond to individuals directly except on your instructions.

These commitments are governed by Indian law (the DPDP Act 2023, the DPDP Rules 2025 and the Indian Contract Act 1872), with arbitration seated at Kanpur, India. The signed DPDP agreement is supplementary to your Service Agreement and prevails on data protection.
